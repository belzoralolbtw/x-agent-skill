# x-plan

Consistency beats intensity on X, and most accounts fail at consistency not
because they lack ideas but because they have no system. This skill turns what
the owner already knows into a week they can actually run.

## Before you write

1. Read the voice profile (`templates/voice.md`), or infer the voice from three posts.
2. Ask three things in one batch: what they do, who they are talking to, and
   how many days a week they can honestly show up. Build for the lower number.
   A plan the owner abandons in week two is worse than a small plan kept.
3. List the raw material they already have: results, mistakes, opinions,
   projects, things they explain often, questions people ask them.

## The week has four kinds of post

Rotate these so the feed is not all one shape. This is the whole plan.

```
Signals   a specific result, number, or thing that happened. Proof and story.
Stances   an opinion or a correction. Pulls replies, builds a point of view.
Lessons   one thing you know that the reader does not. Teaches, gets saved.
Replies   15-30 minutes a day replying to the accounts in the niche (x-reply).
           Not a post, but the growth engine, so it goes on the plan.
```

A simple week at 3 posts a week: one signal, one stance, one lesson, plus the
daily reply block. At 5 posts, add a second lesson and one repurposed post.

## The loop

**1. Fill a one-week grid:** day, post type, the specific idea, the format
(single post or thread). Use the owner's own material, not generic topics.
**2. Write the reply list:** the 5-15 accounts to reply to, and the daily habit
sized to the account (see `x-reply`).
**3. Mark the repurposing slot:** one post a week that reuses something the
owner already published elsewhere (`x-repurpose`).
**4. Give the routine,** not just the calendar. When in the day they write,
when they post, when they reply. The routine is what makes it survive.

## Cadence guidance

```
1-2 a week   fine to start. Better a kept 2 than an abandoned 7.
3-5 a week   the sweet spot for most accounts under 10k.
Daily        only if the owner already has ideas queued. Daily for its own
             sake produces filler, and filler trains the audience to scroll.
```

## Failure modes

- A plan of "post about your niche" with no specific ideas. That is not a plan.
- Seven posts a week for someone who posts once a month. It collapses in a week.
- All four post types collapsed into "tips". The feed becomes monotone.
- No reply block. Without it the plan has no way to reach new people.
- Topics the owner cannot speak to with a real example. If there is no story or
  number behind it, cut it from the week.
