# x-reply

Replies are the growth engine on X. Posts reach the people who already follow
you. Replies reach the audience of the account you reply to, which for a
small account is where all the new people are. This skill is the one that
matters most and the one most people skip.

## Before you write

1. Read the voice profile (`templates/voice.md`), or infer the voice from three of the
   user's posts.
2. Ask who they are trying to reach: the niche, and the 5-15 accounts whose
   audience they want. A reply is aimed at an audience, not at a person.
3. Get the target post pasted in full. A reply to a headline you have not read
   is a reply that lands wrong and costs the account a follower.

## What makes a reply work

A reply earns three things, in this order of value:

- **a profile click** - the reader taps your name because the reply made them
  want to know who wrote it. This is the actual goal.
- **a reply back** - it opens a conversation and puts you in that account's
  replies again tomorrow.
- **a like** - the weakest outcome, and the one most people optimize for.

Rules that follow from that:

- **Add a thing the original did not have.** A number, a counter-example, a
  specific experience, a correction. Agreement alone ("this", "100%", "great
  point") adds nothing and reads as a bot.
- **Be early.** A reply 20 minutes after a big account posts is seen by
  thousands. The same reply four hours later is seen by no one. Speed beats
  polish. This is the whole strategy.
- **Short.** One or two lines. A paragraph in the replies is a post in the
  wrong place.
- **No link, no ask.** Never link your own thing in a reply to a stranger. It
  reads as farming and gets muted.
- **Disagree well or add well.** The two shapes that work: "here is the
  missing piece" and "this was also true when I {specific experience}".
- **Stay in your lane.** Reply in your niche. Random replies to whatever is
  trending build followers who do not want what you sell.

## The shapes

```
Add data      "This is also true in {your domain}: {specific number}."
Add a case    "{Thing} happened to me too - {one specific detail}."
Refine        "The part that gets missed: {refinement}."
Respectful no  "I used to think this. Then {specific event} changed it."
Question      "{A question only the author could answer, that the post raised}."
```

## The loop

**1. Take the target post** pasted by the user.
**2. Write three replies**, one in each of three different shapes above, each
under 200 characters, no links, no asks. Say which one you would post and why.
**3. Humanize** them (`x-human`). Replies are where AI phrasing is most obvious,
because real replies are messy and short.
**4. Give the plan.** Size the daily habit to the account:

```
0-1k followers     Reply to 15-20 accounts a day, first 30 minutes after they post.
1k-10k             Reply to 10 a day, plus 1 post and 1 reply to your own replies.
10k+               Reply to 5 a day to keep the network warm, and post more.
```

## Failure modes

- "Great post!" or an emoji. Nothing for the reader to click on.
- A reply that is actually a post: three sentences, a claim, a call to action.
- Replying to a post you did not read, and repeating a point already in it.
- Farming a big account off-niche for the numbers, then wondering why the
  followers do not convert.
- Replying only to your friends. That is a group chat, not growth.
- Arguing to win. A reply that makes the author look bad closes the door.

## When not to act

Do not write replies that pretend to be a bystander when the account is
promoting something, and do not invent a personal experience the user did not
have. If they want to mention their own product, that belongs on their own
timeline, not in a stranger's replies.
