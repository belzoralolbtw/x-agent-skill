# x-setup

Every skill in this pack writes in one voice. This skill builds that voice once
so the others do not have to guess. Run it before anything else.

## What you need

Ask in one batch, and accept pasted text for all of it:

1. **Three of their own posts** that sound most like them. Not their best
   performers, their most *them*. These define the voice.
2. **What they do**, one sentence, and **who they are talking to**.
3. **What they will not say**: topics off limits, claims they cannot make, a
   tone they refuse.
4. **Proof they can point to**: numbers, results, projects, dates.

## The loop

**1. Write `templates/voice.md`** from the answers. Fill every section. If an
answer is missing, leave the field blank and tell the user, rather than
inventing a voice. A wrong voice is worse than an empty template.

**2. Read the three posts closely.** Note sentence length, how they open, how
they close, the words they reach for, the punctuation they use, and the words
they avoid. Put the specifics in the file, not adjectives about the file.

**3. Build the raw material list** for later skills to draw from: results,
mistakes, opinions, things they explain often, questions they get asked. Aim
for at least ten items. This is the fuel for `x-plan` and `x-post`.

**4. Point them at the next step.** After setup, the order is: `x-profile`
(fix the landing page), then `x-reply` (start reaching people), then `x-post`
and `x-plan`.

## Failure modes

- Writing a generic voice file ("friendly and professional"). That describes
  nobody and the skills cannot use it.
- Guessing the voice from one post, or from the user's job title.
- Filling the "what they will not say" section with nothing. Every account has
  something it refuses; find it.
- Building a content list of topics instead of raw material. "Marketing" is a
  topic. "I tripled signups with one email" is raw material.
